# Buried caves and swimming — 0.5.9 preview

The Hollow of the Witnesses, Ashfang and Wyrmcrag now sit within terrain-covered hills. Their entrances lead into rock at the foot of the slopes. Nearby vegetation follows the raised ground, and the old exposed rectangular backing shells no longer supply collision.

The hidden chamber behind Widow's Veil and all three cave areas share a continuous walking network: 2.78 kilometres of centreline across 19 routes, two large loops and four dead-end branches. Sloping corridors descend beneath the vale, with rounded rock surfaces, stone footsteps and small mineral-lit junctions. Existing puzzle rooms, witnesses, relics and reward requirements remain. This adds routes and exploration space, not new quest chains or encounters along every corridor.

The six old interact-to-teleport markers have been removed. Walk through the openings to travel between caves. The original 99-cell labyrinth remains, and the waterfall walkway is physically clear. Coloured mineral lights distinguish junctions; the lantern is useful in the longer dark passages.

The pool below the waterfall now supports **surface swimming**. Flotation is automatic. Use the normal movement keys to swim, Sprint to swim faster and Jump to push upward near a bank. Weapons are lowered while swimming. Visual water/foam meshes have explicit non-solid collision profiles, and the western bank has a clear opening to walk out. This update covers the receiving pool; it does not introduce free diving, an oxygen system or swimming throughout every river and lake.

288 packaged campaign, runtime, combat and swimming checks passed. Cave clearance sampled 4,257 player-sized positions along the network and waterfall approach. A separate movement test walked the complete 384.0-metre Hollow descent in 124.6 seconds using normal movement input. The pool test fell into the water, floated, swam toward the western bank and returned to walking. Seven packaged screenshots were inspected. These checks do not replace a full ordinary-play campaign or every possible off-route exploration attempt. The exterior mouths still show angular rock-to-terrain joins in places; burying and connecting the caves is a functional improvement, not final environment-art acceptance.

Installation preserved 4 existing save/settings files byte-for-byte. The save format is unchanged. No purchases, paid services or new asset downloads were needed; existing creators retain their full credits. The runtime ZIP links to historical screenshots online instead of including every old image, reducing duplicate download/storage use. Licensed runtime content and the reusable livestock adaptations retain their existing notices.
