Frostwatch adaptations: legacy COLLADA repairs; cattle/pig silhouette subdivision and smooth shading; sheep parent-transform normalization; selection of the rigged wool variant; material reconnection; conversion of idle, walking and feeding animation clips to FBX; extraction/conversion of the associated base-colour images. The selected sheep source used 24 fps; cattle and pig exports use 30 fps. In-game scale and playback rates are configured separately. Adaptations are released under the same CC BY-SA 3.0 licence. Original authors do not endorse Frostwatch.
