# Frostwatch player acceptance test

Use an existing willing tester and existing hardware; no paid service or purchase is required. Do not overwrite a personal playthrough. Back up the complete Saved folder before using a fresh test save. Record the build version, operating system, CPU, GPU/VRAM, RAM, storage type, resolution and preset. Do not publish account details or raw personal files.

## First hour — no coaching

Start a new expedition. Read or skip the opening as you normally would. Play for an hour without being told which path to take. The observer should not explain solutions. Record where the player stops, retraces steps, misses an interaction, cannot explain the objective or becomes frustrated. Distinguish a deliberately mysterious story from unclear controls or missing guidance.

After the session ask: What are you trying to achieve? Who matters to that goal? What made you want to explore? Why did the last combat hit land? What would make you stop playing? What would you choose to do next? Record the player's own words rather than suggesting positive answers.

## Complete journey

Continue from Hearthmere through Rowan, the woodland encounter, Widow's Veil, the Abbey and Frostwatch to the ending. Keep a checkpoint diary of actual route, elapsed play time, current objective, acquired rewards and any blocked or confusing steps. Do not use diagnostic teleportation, console commands, forced flags or invulnerability to count this as a completed playthrough.

Test an older save separately. Confirm that completed quests remain complete, rescued characters and the ending dialogue agree with progress, and defeated bosses do not reappear. Ordinary enemies should follow the documented respawn delay and arrival restrictions.

## Reliability and comfort

- Save/quit/relaunch at a camp, after a quest reward, while mounted and after the ending. Check inventory, objectives and location.
- Die during an ordinary encounter and a boss encounter. Check recovery and retained progression.
- Switch display settings, let an unwanted mode revert, then restart. Check that chosen settings persist.
- Read a long page and dialogue at each supported text size and at 960×540/720p/1080p where the hardware permits. Check page controls and subtitles.
- Disable page motion. Check that entering dialogue and turning pages require no animation delay.
- Try every advertised input action. Controller support requires its own navigation and gameplay pass before it can be advertised as complete.
- Listen for looping seams, clipped speech, incorrect pronunciation, excessive repetition and sounds unrelated to the action or surface.
- Travel, ride and fight for a sustained session. Record stutters, loading delays, invisible assets, collision traps and any decline as the laptop warms up.

## Issue record

For each issue capture: build, location, exact steps, expected outcome, actual outcome, whether it repeats, screenshot/video if available, and whether progress was lost. Classify as progression/data loss, gameplay/comfort, performance or presentation. Fix and retest progression/data-loss problems before release. No passing automated check overrides a reproducible player problem.

Acceptance requires real observations. This brief is preparation, not evidence that external tests have happened.
