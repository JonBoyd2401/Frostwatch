# Castle structure and prison — 0.5.5 preview

The prison now has iron grilles, masonry framing and a hinged gate. Defeating Ste Chlomaine opens the gate; an existing rescued-Edrin save also keeps it open. A fresh expedition closes it. A gallery above the main entrance links the gate towers visually, with actual arrow-slit openings, parapets and projecting supports. Low storage bays define an edge of the courtyard while keeping the centre clear. Exterior castle accents fade out in daylight. The prison's practical lamps use 220 lumens and a 650 cm radius, with its exterior clue light reduced to 180 lumens at night; the great hall retains its interior lighting.

This is a focused architectural pass. It does not complete the castle, add an accessible wall-walk, replace the keep, or resolve terrain repetition and road edges. Existing creator materials and character assets are reused. No new lights, downloads or paid services were needed. Six added actors total 1,164 triangles.

252 packaged checks passed, including gate presence, blocking entry before defeat, capsule clearance after defeat, legacy rescued-save compatibility, fresh-game reset and daylight/prison lighting behaviour. The live parry/counter test also passed. Daylight, night, aerial and open-gate captures passed resource/render checks and were visually reviewed before installation. Initial captures exposed excessive prison brightness and daytime accent lighting; those captures were retained and the correction was reverified. These are regression checks, not a complete castle playthrough or external player acceptance.
